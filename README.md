## About
Prism Pong is a fast paced arcade game that reimagines the classic Pong formula.

Defend your goal, score points, and survive as long as possible while the game becomes increasingly chaotic. More balls enter the arena over time, their speed increases, and every mistake becomes more costly.

Beat your high score and see how long you can survive.  

## Features
- Modern arcade twist on classic Pong
- Increasing difficulty through additional balls and rising game speed
- Arcade gameplay featuring a health system
- Recover health through level ups
- High score focused gameplay
- Earn more points the longer you survive 

## Tech
- Unity
- C#
